================================================================
1. TOM TAT NGAN
================================================================

Day la ung dung web/desktop local de dua anh vao model AI va nhan anh
da duoc xu ly. Nguoi dung co the quan ly model, chay suy luan (inference),
xem lich su va tai ket qua.

Can phan biet ro:

- Day la ung dung SU DUNG model de suy luan.
- Trong source code hien tai khong thay training loop de huan luyen model.
- Dataset builder chi tao anh cap clean/degraded, khong tu huan luyen model.
- Ung dung khong doc noi dung chu nhu OCR. No xu ly anh pixel; khong tu
  chuyen chu viet thanh van ban.
- Model duoc ho tro tu dong hien tai la Restormer checkpoint.

Cong nghe chinh:
- Frontend: React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS.
- Backend: Python, FastAPI, PyTorch.
- Luu tai khoan, session, metadata model, lich su: MySQL.
- Luu file anh va checkpoint: cac thu muc tren may dang chay backend.
- Desktop wrapper: Electron.

================================================================
2. CAC KHAI NIEM CAN BIET
================================================================

Frontend:
Phan giao dien chay trong browser/Electron. Hien thi form, nhan thao tac,
gui request den backend, va hien thi tien trinh/ket qua.

Backend:
Server Python nhan API request, kiem tra dang nhap/quyen, luu file,
nap model PyTorch, chay suy luan, tra ket qua.

API:
Giao dien giao tiep giua frontend va backend. Vi du frontend gui anh va
model_id den POST /api/inference.

Checkpoint (.pth, .pt):
File chua trong so (weights) cua model. Thong thuong khong chua day du
ma nguon hay dataset. Backend dung kien truc Restormer trong source de
tao model, sau do nap weights vao model.

Inference:
Dung model da duoc huan luyen de tao ket qua tu anh dau vao. Khac voi
training la qua trinh cap nhat weights tu du lieu va loss.

Tensor:
Du lieu anh da chuyen thanh dang ma PyTorch tinh toan duoc. Trong ung dung,
anh RGB uint8 duoc dua ve float [0,1], doi thu tu tu NHWC sang NCHW.

GPU/CUDA:
CUDA la nen tang tinh toan GPU NVIDIA. Che do Auto chon CUDA neu PyTorch
nhan thay GPU CUDA; neu khong thi chon CPU. Full-image va batch lon co the
can nhieu VRAM hon.

Tile:
Mieng anh nho cat ra tu anh lon. Chay model theo tung mieng giup giam RAM/
VRAM. Tile overlap la phan chong len nhau giua cac mieng de tron bien tile,
giam duong noi trong anh dau ra.

PSNR va SSIM:
Chi so so sanh hai anh:
- PSNR (dB): do sai lech pixel; thuong cao hon la tot hon.
- SSIM: do tuong dong cau truc; gan 1 thuong la tuong dong cao.
Chi so chi co y nghia chat luong khi co anh ground truth phu hop.

4. LUONG CHAY TOAN UNG DUNG
================================================================

A. Khoi dong

1. start.bat doc APP_PORT tu backend/.env.
2. start.bat chay backend/start.ps1.
3. start.ps1 dung uv de chay app.server.
4. app.server khoi dong Uvicorn tai app_host/app_port.
5. Khi app khoi dong, main.py tao cac thu muc va khoi tao bang MySQL.
6. Neu frontend/dist ton tai, backend phuc vu giao dien da build.
7. start.bat mo Microsoft Edge o dia chi local.

Che do phat trien frontend:
- Vite doc VITE_DEV_HOST, VITE_DEV_PORT, VITE_API_BASE_URL tu backend/.env.
- Request /api duoc Vite proxy sang backend.

Desktop:
- Electron mo app trong cua so rieng va co the tu khoi dong backend.
- Can chu y: desktop/main.js hien kiem tra backend o port 8000 va tim Python
  trong backend/.venv. Trong khi backend/start.ps1 dung uv va app port doc tu
  .env. Neu cac gia tri/moi truong nay khong khop, desktop co the khong chay
  dung nhu mong doi.

B. Dang nhap

1. Frontend goi GET /api/auth/status.
2. Backend tra setup_required va user neu cookie session hop le.
3. Neu chua co admin, frontend hien man hinh tao tai khoan admin dau tien.
4. Khi login thanh cong, server tao token ngau nhien, chi luu hash token trong
   MySQL va gui token goc trong cookie HttpOnly.
5. Cac API can dang nhap doc cookie va tra user tu session.
6. require_user kiem tra dang nhap; require_admin kiem tra role admin.

Password duoc hash bang scrypt voi salt rieng. Dung session cookie HttpOnly
de JavaScript frontend khong doc truc tiep token.

C. Chay inference

1. Nguoi dung chon model va upload/chon anh tren Dashboard.
2. Frontend gui multipart/form-data len POST /api/inference, bao gom:
   model_id, input_image, tile_size, tile_overlap, batch_size, device,
   processing_mode, degradation_mode va tham so degradation.
3. Backend kiem tra:
   - nguoi dung da dang nhap;
   - model ton tai;
   - file anh co extension duoc chap nhan va dung gioi han;
   - tile_size >= 32 va chia het cho 8;
   - overlap nho hon tile_size;
   - batch_size tu 1 den 64;
   - mode/device hop le.
4. File anh duoc luu voi UUID, khong dung ten file client lam duong dan.
5. Backend tao job status queued, roi len lich chay qua FastAPI BackgroundTasks.
6. API tra job_id cho frontend.
7. Frontend poll GET /api/inference/{job_id} moi khoang 800ms.
8. Backend cap nhat job trong bo nho: queued -> loading_model -> processing
   -> completed hoac failed.
9. Frontend hien progress, tile count, thoi gian va ket qua.
10. Khi thanh cong, anh output duoc luu trong outputs/; metadata history vao
    MySQL. Frontend tai anh qua API outputs co kiem tra quyen.

Luu y: trang thai job dang chay nam trong bo nho backend, khong phai task queue
phan tan. Neu restart backend giua luc chay, job dang xu ly co the mat.

5. PIPELINE XU LY ANH VA MODEL
================================================================

Pipeline trong inference/pipeline.py:

1. Mo anh bang Pillow, xu ly EXIF orientation, chuyen RGB.
2. Tien xu ly anh chup dien thoai bang OpenCV:
   - thu nho anh de tim duong vien nhanh hon;
   - neu tim thay nhieu tu giay, tach tung trang va chinh phoi canh tren anh goc;
   - uoc luong nen va chia anh cho nen de giam bong do tren tung trang;
   - chuyen anh xam thanh RGB 3 kenh. Neu khong tim thay vien ro rang,
     giu nguyen khung anh va van chuan hoa anh sang.
   Khong ep anh ve 1024x1024 de tranh meo ti le va mat chi tiet. Moi trang
   duoc chay inference, luu, phan quyen va hien thi rieng trong ket qua/history.
3. Neu degradation_mode = direct:
   - dua anh upload vao model;
   - khong co ground truth that.
4. Neu chon mot degradation mode khac direct:
   - coi anh da tien xu ly la anh clean;
   - tao ban noisy/blurred/broken/missing/occluded;
   - dua ban suy giam vao model;
   - so sanh ket qua voi anh clean ban dau.
5. ModelLoader doc checkpoint bang PyTorch.
6. ModelLoader nhan dien state_dict va chi ho tro Restormer.
   No co the tim state_dict trong mot so key thong dung va loai bo prefix.
7. Tham so mang nhu so channel, block, attention head duoc suy ra tu weights.
8. Restormer duoc tao va load_state_dict(strict=True).
9. Anh duoc chay tren CUDA hoac CPU.
10. Ket qua duoc clip ve [0,1], doi ve RGB uint8 va ghi PNG.

Restormer la mang encoder-decoder:
- Encoder giam kich thuoc khong gian va tang channel.
- Latent la tang o do sau nhat.
- Decoder tang kich thuoc va ket hop skip connections tu encoder.
- Transformer block gom attention va feed-forward network.
- Output cong them input trong cau hinh Restormer thong thuong (residual).

Tile mode:
- Chia anh thanh tile co overlap.
- Padding reflect o canh anh de giu pixel canh tu nhien.
- Chay model tung batch tile.
- Ghep tile bang feather weighting: vung giua tile co trong so cao, bien overlap
  duoc pha tron.
- Cat ve kich thuoc anh goc.

Full-image mode:
- Chay ca anh trong mot lan.
- Padding chieu cao/rong len boi so 8 do Restormer downsample 3 lan.
- Cat ve kich thuoc ban dau.
- Anh lon co the het VRAM/RAM; tile mode an toan hon.

Batch size lon thuong co the tang toc GPU nhung ton VRAM hon.
Tile size lon co the giu nhieu context hon nhung ton bo nho hon.
Overlap lon tang phan tinh toan nhung co the giam duong noi giua tile.

================================================================
6. DEGRADATION VA CAC CHI SO
================================================================

Cac degradation mode:
- direct: chay thang anh that vao model.
- synthetic_noise: them Gaussian noise.
- blur: lam mo bang Gaussian blur.
- broken: tao doan dut tren net chu.
- missing: xoa cac vung anh.
- occlusion: che mot so vung bang khoi mau.

Trong synthetic mode, anh upload duoc coi la clean ground truth.
Ung dung tao degraded input va co the tinh PSNR/SSIM voi clean reference.
Day la cach thu pipeline co biet dap an.

Trong direct mode, ung dung khong co anh dap an. PSNR/SSIM vs input chi cho biet
output khac input bao nhieu; KHONG chung minh output chinh xac hon.

Giao dien co "Confidence Score", nhung day la diem tu tinh tu PSNR/SSIM:
55% PSNR da chuan hoa + 45% SSIM da chuan hoa. No khong phai xac suat,
khong phai confidence that cua model, va khong nen xem nhu diem danh gia khoa hoc.

- Do do chi co train split; chua co nguon rieng cho validation/test.
- Lap lai nhieu kieu suy giam tu cung mot trang khong tuong duong voi co nhieu
  trang/doc gia khac nhau. Khong nen dua ra ket luan model tong quat tu dataset
  nay.

Chia split theo anh goc, khong theo tung ban suy giam, de tranh anh cung mot
trang vua xuat hien trong train vua xuat hien trong test.

Lenh tao dataset (PowerShell, chay trong backend):

  python -m app.inference.dataset_builder `

QUAN TRONG:

15. BAO VE MAT KHAU VA DU LIEU KHI TRUYEN QUA MANG
================================================================

Mat khau tai khoan:
- Backend da KHONG luu password goc. auth_service.py dung scrypt voi salt
  ngau nhien de luu password hash.
- Hash password chi bao ve du lieu trong database neu database bi doc.
  No KHONG ma hoa request HTTP. Neu dang gui qua HTTP, nguoi bat duoc traffic
  co the doc password dang nhap, cookie session, anh va checkpoint.
- Khong tu viet ma hoa password o frontend thay cho HTTPS; ma hoa tuy bien
  khong bao ve cac file upload va token session.

HTTPS/TLS da duoc them vao:
- Backend server.py doc TLS_CERTFILE va TLS_KEYFILE de bat HTTPS.
- Vite dev server cung dung cap chung certificate/key neu duoc cau hinh.
- Electron doc APP_PORT va cap giao thuc HTTP/HTTPS tu backend/.env.
- Khi bat TLS, cau hinh bat buoc SESSION_COOKIE_SECURE=true.
- Backend khong cho APP_HOST bind ra interface khong-loopback neu chua bat TLS.
- Vite dev server cung chi duoc bind localhost neu chua bat TLS.
- Certificate phai duoc may khach tin cay va SAN phai khop hostname/IP nguoi
  dung truy cap. Khong tat kiem tra certificate de "cho no chay".
- Cau hinh hien tai trong backend/.env dang de APP_HOST va VITE_DEV_HOST la
  127.0.0.1, khong co certificate TLS, va SESSION_COOKIE_SECURE=false.
  Nghia la hien chay HTTP chi tren may local; LAN khong the ket noi toi server.
  Muon dung qua LAN/Internet phai cai certificate tin cay, bat TLS, dat cookie
  secure va cap nhat cac origin/URL theo HTTPS truoc khi mo bind ra mang.

Cac buoc bat HTTPS:
1. Xin certificate tu CA tin cay. Neu chi dung trong LAN, tao certificate
   tu CA noi bo va cai CA goc vao trust store cua may khach.
2. Dat certificate va private key tren backend, gioi han quyen doc private key.
3. Trong backend/.env, dat:
   TLS_CERTFILE=certs/server-cert.pem
   TLS_KEYFILE=certs/server-key.pem
   SESSION_COOKIE_SECURE=true
4. Certificate can co SAN cho dung ten/IP truy cap. Electron mac dinh ket noi
   https://127.0.0.1:APP_PORT, nen certificate desktop can co SAN IP 127.0.0.1.
5. Neu chay frontend bang Vite, doi VITE_API_BASE_URL thanh https://...,
   doi FRONTEND_ORIGIN va CORS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS sang dung origin https,
   va dat VITE_DEV_HOST/PORT phu hop certificate.
6. Khoi dong lai server. Truy cap bang https:// va kiem tra trinh duyet bao
   ket noi an toan, khong bo qua canh bao certificate.

MySQL:

- MYSQL_SSL_CA tro den CA certificate cua MySQL server; code bat ca xac minh
  certificate va hostname. Khong dung CA khong tin cay.
- Neu MySQL chay tren cung may, ket noi localhost khong di qua mang.
